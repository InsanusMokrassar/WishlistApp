The current implementation has completed the separation from User but assigns the complete pending state to email/common. Correct that ownership with a dedicated serializable UsersFeatureEmailProfile in features/users/common/src/commonMain/kotlin/models/UsersFeatureEmailProfile.kt. Rename and relocate the current EmailProfile rather than adding a second model and conversion layer.
