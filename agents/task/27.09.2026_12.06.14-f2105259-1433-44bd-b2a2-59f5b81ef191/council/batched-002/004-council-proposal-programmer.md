Issue P1: preserving arbitrary historical Long timestamps exactly is impossible with this mandated Double-backed representation. Current code accepts much larger cooldowns than exact millisecond precision permits. An unchecked DateTime conversion could round a persisted deadline downward and admit a mutation early, or collapse distinct request snapshots. Recommend explicit validation of supported persisted/generated instants and checked storage conversion, with a documented fail-closed path for unsupported legacy values. Ordinary current epoch milliseconds are within the exact range. Do not silently claim full Long-domain compatibility or introduce a new range limit without acknowledging the policy change.
