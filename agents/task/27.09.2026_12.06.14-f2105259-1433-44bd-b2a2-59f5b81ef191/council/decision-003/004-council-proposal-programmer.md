## Alternatives and compatibility

Exact preservation of arbitrary Long timestamp identity inside DateTime plus the mandated serializer is impossible. Retaining parallel Long state, a wrapper instant, or a custom serializer conflicts with the requested uniform representation or serializer. Silently rounding/clamping values changes deadline admission and feedback identity and is rejected. A ±2^53 integer-only policy offers more exact integer values but exceeds the pinned library's documented lossless contract and offers no relevant product benefit. A contemporary-date restriction would be narrower than necessary. The documented ±2^52 integral policy is preferred.

Wire compatibility is directional: new clients accept existing integral numeric timestamp input within the accepted domain; new output uses Double numeric serialization. Strict old Long decoders may reject decimal or exponent syntax even for mathematically integral values. Do not claim mixed-version interoperability from numeric equivalence. Deploy matching server and clients together, invalidate old web assets, and retain the existing no-mixed-writers lifecycle requirement.

Before activation perform a read-only database count for each non-null email timestamp below -4503599627370496 or above 4503599627370496, with a combined affected-row count. Read-only checks must inspect raw BIGINT values, without casting through Double. Stop deployment when unsupported rows exist; preserve the backup and rows, report counts without email addresses, and prepare separately authorized repair or compatible backport. No automatic repair belongs to this task. No timestamp schema rewrite is required. Timestamp-only rollback to the immediately preceding compatible lifecycle release is data-preserving for in-range BIGINT rows, but older lifecycle-incompatible binaries remain unsafe; retain the existing backup/restore or compatible-backport recovery guidance and disclose backup data loss.
