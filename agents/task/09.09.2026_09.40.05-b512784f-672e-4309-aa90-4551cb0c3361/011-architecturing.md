The existing deeplinks service owns UUID allocation, persistence, lookup, and exact removal. UI/users owns the owner action and pending/completed form; client owns navigation. No new feature, persistent store, token format, distributed lock, transaction framework, route family, or production dependency is authorized.

The authenticated POST /api/auth/requestPasswordChangeEmail accepts only expectedEmail; the bearer subject is authoritative. A request must still find that subject, the exact current non-null approved address, direct UserRole authorization, a stored password, configured SMTP, and available deeplinks. Root receives no ability to issue on behalf of another user. The public POST /api/auth/completePasswordChange accepts the unchanged userId assertion, exact approvalId, and unmodified password. Missing roles infrastructure, missing credentials, and account removal remain fail-closed.
